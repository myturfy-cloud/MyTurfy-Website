Refund & Cancellation

Our Refund Policy

We believe in fair treatment for both players and venue owners. Read how our refund process works.

Last updated: July 2026

How Your Payment is Held

When you complete a booking on MyTurfy, your payment is collected and held securely by MyTurfy — it does not go directly to the venue owner at the time of booking. This is intentional and works in your favour.

You payPayment collected securely via Razorpay and held by MyTurfy
Slot time arrivesYour booking runs as planned at the venue
Payment released to ownerOnly after your slot has completed with no active refund request

This escrow-style flow means you have a real window to raise a dispute before any money reaches the venue owner.

When You Can Request a Refund

✅ Eligible for Refund

  • Venue was closed or unavailable at your booked time
  • Venue conditions were significantly different from what was listed
  • Technical issue caused a double-booking
  • You cancel more than 24 hours before your slot
  • The venue owner cancels your booking

❌ Not Eligible for Refund

  • No-show (you didn't turn up for your slot)
  • Cancellation within 24 hours of your slot time
  • Change of plans or personal reasons after the slot has started
  • Dissatisfaction with the sport result (wins/losses are not grounds for refund)

How to Request a Refund

1
Log into your MyTurfy account

Go to your booking history and find the relevant booking.

2
Click "Request Refund"

Provide a brief reason for your refund request. This is important — it helps the venue owner and our support team review your case fairly.

3
Emails are sent automatically

Your refund request is emailed to both the venue owner and MyTurfy support at the same time. You don't need to contact anyone separately.

4
Owner reviews within 48 hours

The venue owner can approve or reject your request via their partner dashboard.

5
If approved — refund processed

₹100% of the booking amount is refunded to your original payment method within 5–7 business days. The venue owner receives nothing for this booking.

6
If rejected — escalate to MyTurfy

If the owner rejects your request and you believe it's unfair, email us at myturfy@gmail.com with your booking ID. We will review independently and can override the owner's decision if warranted.

Refund processing times are governed by Razorpay and your bank, not MyTurfy. Once we initiate the refund, we have no control over when it appears in your account.
